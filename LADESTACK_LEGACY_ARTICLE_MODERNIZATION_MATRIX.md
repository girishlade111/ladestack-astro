# LadeStack — Legacy Article Modernization Matrix (Phase 3C)

This matrix establishes the comprehensive editorial audit, lifecycle tracking, and modernization roadmap for all twenty-seven (27) legacy English blog articles originally published under the `blogPosts.ts` and `blogContent.ts` system.

---

## 1. Migration Policy & Guiding Principles

1. **Zero Broken Canonical URLs:** Every existing production URL (`https://ladestack.in/blog/<slug>`) must remain functional. Never mass-delete or break URLs.
2. **Selective Same-URL Modernization (`REWRITE_SAME_URL`):** When modernizing an article, the exact original English slug is preserved in Astro Content Collections (`src/content/blog/en/<slug>.md`). The legacy generator dynamically excludes that slug, seamlessly serving the rewritten Markdown article with native Astro rendering and zero duplicate routes.
3. **No Wholesale Scraping or Machine Mass-Rewrites:** Articles are individually researched, fact-checked, rewritten with authentic executable code, and updated with honest metadata (`publishedAt` preserved from original date, `updatedAt` stamped on actual revision date).
4. **Controlled Multilingual Localization:** Only the highest-tier, universally applicable modernized articles (Batch 1: 2 articles) are expanded into the 8 non-English locales (`ja`, `ko`, `zh`, `ru`, `tr`, `fr`, `pt`, `es`). Niche or English-only articles emit zero false hreflangs.
5. **Temporary Retention of Stable Legacy Fallbacks (`RETAIN_TEMPORARILY`):** The remaining 22 legacy articles continue to resolve via the Astro legacy route fallback until their scheduled modernization batch.
