# LadeStack — Technical Claim Evidence Register (Phase 3C)

This document establishes a rigorous, reproducible editorial verification system and fact-checking audit trail for all technical, numerical, performance, benchmark, and legal compliance assertions across LadeStack's multilingual publications.

---

## 1. Editorial Claim Verification Methodology

Every factual, numerical, and architectural assertion in LadeStack publications is categorized under one of seven definitive statuses:

- `VERIFIED`: Confirmed by direct experimental reproduction, mathematical calculation, or live system inspection.
- `SUPPORTED_BY_DOCUMENTATION`: Confirmed by official, authoritative technical standards (W3C, IETF, ECMA, MDN Web Docs, RFC).
- `MEASURED`: Directly recorded from repeatable benchmark fixtures with documented hardware, OS, and toolchains.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE`: Pedagogical or conceptual model whose numerical assumptions are explicitly defined and labeled as non-empirical reference estimates.
- `UNVERIFIED`: Lacks empirical verification or formal documentation; queued for correction or removal.
- `INCORRECT`: Mathematically false, specification-violating, or legally non-compliant; corrected or replaced immediately.
- `REMOVED`: Deleted from publication due to lack of factual foundation.
